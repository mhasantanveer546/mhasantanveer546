## Projects

### Learnio

Learnio is an AI-powered study assistant designed for university students. It helps students work with their study material through features such as summaries, quizzes, flashcards, and AI-powered conversations with their notes.

**Technologies:** Python, Flask, PostgreSQL, AI APIs

### Ember

Ember is a personal search and knowledge management project. The idea is to make it easier to organize and search through personal documents, notes, and other information.

The project also gives me an opportunity to apply Data Structures and Algorithms concepts to a practical system.

**Technologies:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL

### MeetMind

MeetMind is an AI-based meeting system that focuses on more than just generating meeting summaries. It is designed to keep track of commitments, follow-ups, and information discussed across different meetings.

**Technologies:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, pgvector, AI/LLM technologies
